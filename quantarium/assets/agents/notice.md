# Before you turn on agent features

Agent features let an AI model work in this Quantarium workspace: reading your cells, scripts, scenarios, and results; writing strategy scripts and cell drafts; running simulations; and proposing changes for you to approve. They are off until you turn them on. Please read this first.

Nothing Quantarium shows, and nothing a connected AI model says or writes, is investment, financial, legal, or tax advice. Simulated and forward-tested results do not predict real results.

## Your data goes to the AI provider you choose

When agent features are on, content from this workspace (strategy code, cell settings, results, file names, and anything else an agent reads) is sent to the AI provider you connect. That provider's terms and privacy policy govern it. Quantarium's authors never receive it, and Quantarium has no telemetry.

## Your accounts, their terms, and their costs

You are responsible for using your AI provider's and your broker's services within their terms, including any rules on how subscriptions may be used, and for any charges they make.

## AI models can be wrong or manipulated

Models make mistakes, and text they read (a scenario description, a script comment, a file header) can try to steer them. Quantarium limits what an agent can do and asks you to approve consequential actions. These limits reduce the risk; they do not remove it. Read what an agent proposes before you approve it.

## Keep your keys out of chats and files

Keep API keys and broker credentials in your system keychain or environment variables. Never paste them into a chat, a strategy script, or a workspace file.

## Turning it off

You can turn agent features off at any time in Quantarium (Agents, then Turn off agent features) or with `quantarium agents disable`. Turning them off stops every agent connection.
